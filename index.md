# Privacy Policy – Messenger N8N

Last Updated: November 21, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Messenger N8N is an automated customer communication
service that connects Facebook Messenger with n8n
automation workflows and AI-powered responses.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is
processed when users interact with our Messenger service.

## 2. Information We Process

Depending on the features enabled, we may process:

- Facebook Messenger message content.
- Sender and Page identifiers.
- Message timestamps.
- Images, audio, or other attachments sent by users,
  if these are processed by the workflow.

## 3. How We Use Information

Information is processed to:

- Receive and respond to Messenger messages.
- Provide automated customer support.
- Generate AI-assisted responses.
- Execute requested automation workflows.
- Maintain service security and reliability.

## 4. Third-Party Services

Our service uses Meta Messenger Platform and n8n
to receive and process messages.

Message content may also be processed by the
AI provider configured in our workflow.

The applicable providers and their privacy policies
should be identified in this section.

## 5. Data Storage and Retention

Information may be stored in n8n workflow executions,
memory systems, or databases depending on the
configuration of the service.

Data is retained only for as long as necessary
for the stated purposes, subject to applicable
legal requirements.

## 6. Data Sharing

Information may be processed by the service providers
required to operate the Messenger integration,
including Meta, n8n hosting infrastructure,
and the configured AI provider.

We do not authorize unrelated parties to use
this information for their own purposes.
